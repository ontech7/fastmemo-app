## 🖥 Desktop 2.2.1

- 30 new category icons for everyday topics: family, food, health, travel, pets, bills and more, suggested as you type the name.
  - Family, work, food, coffee, shopping, health, gym, sport, bike, car, flights, travel, events, pets, plants, clothes, video
    games, news, legal, art, weather, beach, mountains, baby, dentist, wine, bills, TV series, languages and hobbies.
  - Typing a category's name suggests the matching icons in all seven languages of the app.
  - A category made with one of these icons on a phone now shows it here too.
- "All" always comes first in the category bar, and on the Organize page it has its own icon and a lock.
  - The Organize page's "All" shows the same icon as the category bar, and the lock says it stays first.
- Creating a category: the confirm button is now at the top and no longer covers the icons.
